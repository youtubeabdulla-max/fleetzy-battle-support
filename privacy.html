Fleetzy — Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026 · version 1.0.52

Summary

Fleetzy does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking in the app. Your game progress is stored on your device only.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your progress: coins, gems, level, rank, unlocked cosmetics, settings, mission and achievement state, and the last 30 match results. This lives in the app's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset local data.
  • A random guest identifier (g-…) generated on first launch. It contains no personal information.
  • The display name you choose (defaults to "Captain").
  • Replay images you save with "Replay & share" are written to the app's own folder and are only shared when you share them.

Online play (when a match server is enabled)

If you play online, the app sends your guest identifier, display name, level and your in-game moves to the match server so that an opponent can be found and the match can be refereed. The server keeps this only for the duration of the match. No IP addresses, device identifiers or contacts are stored. In the current release online play is disabled and shows a "coming soon" card.

Advertising and purchases

This release contains no advertising network and no real-money purchases. The "watch ad" placements show a placeholder screen produced by the app itself. If a future update adds an ad or purchase provider, this policy will be updated before that update is released.

Children

Fleetzy is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Permissions

The app requests network access (to reach the match server when online play is enabled) and nothing else: no camera, microphone, contacts, location or photo library access.

Your choices

Use Settings → Reset local data to erase everything the app stored, or uninstall the app.
